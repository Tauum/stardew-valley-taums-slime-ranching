## Prototype status

The Slime Playground is implemented as group-play furniture: it reserves one placed object for two nearby Ranch Slimes and starts their short hop animations together. It has no happiness or friendship effect yet. Marnie's default prototype price is 6,000g, configurable in `config.json`; price and progression remain open for review.

The Slime Slide is also available at a configurable 3,000g prototype price. A nearby Ranch Slime performs a low hop and horizontal glide across it. Its travel direction is currently fixed, and it has no gameplay effect beyond the animation.

The Slime Bed is available at a configurable 1,800g prototype price. One nearby slime settles into a lowered resting pose, holds briefly, and eases back upright. It does not advance age, change happiness, or affect breeding eligibility.

The Compost Trough is a functional juvenile-feeding prototype, sold by Marnie for a configurable 3,500g by default. Use it inside the Slime Daycare, select a juvenile and one eligible monster drop, fishing-trash item, or algae from your inventory, then feed it. A slime may be fed once per in-game day. The provisional base effect advances maturity by one day; the slime's favorite food advances it by one extra day. Growth caps at maturity, and slimes assigned to another ranching job cannot be fed. Feeding and the per-day limit are saved in the external registry alongside normal Stardew inventory and animal saves.

The growth amount, favorite-food bonus, accepted item set, and shop progression need playtesting and review. Each eligible item is consumed when fed; no drops are generated by the trough.

The Slime Tunnel is a decorative crawl-through interaction. Nearby idle Ranch Slimes make a low pass through its clear arch. The animation is transient, gets cleaned up when leaving the Daycare, and does not modify animal position, age, friendship, or genome. Marnie's default test price is 2,500g and is configurable. Ranching jobs reserve animals first, so assigned slimes do not start furniture interactions.

The Heated Slime Stone is a decorative warm resting spot. Nearby idle Ranch Slimes settle beside it for a few seconds, then return to their original pose. It has an ember-glow pixel sprite and a configurable 1,800g test price. It does not change friendship, temperature, health, or maturity; winter-specific comfort mechanics remain undecided.

The Slime Pond is an indoor decorative water feature. Nearby idle slimes bob briefly as if swimming and splashing, then return to their original pose. It has a generated water-basin sprite and a configurable 4,500g test price. No items are produced; whether it should generate rare existing-item drops, and at what capped rate, remains an economy decision.

The Slime Swing is a decorative seat that nearby idle Ranch Slimes can sway on for a few seconds. The animation uses a small reversible horizontal draw offset; it does not move the animal's collision tile or change stats. The generated wooden swing sprite is sold at a configurable 2,200g prototype price.

## Interaction architecture
Furniture exposes a target position, eligible sizes, interaction duration, maximum concurrent users, animation, cooldown and optional happiness delta. A hutch-level scheduler periodically assigns idle slimes to available furniture. Use visual offsets for bouncing rather than moving collision boxes vertically. Reserve targets to avoid multiple slimes deadlocking. Skip or simplify offscreen animations.

**Rule:** Do not make decorative objects mandatory for optimal breeding; favor expressiveness and player customization.
