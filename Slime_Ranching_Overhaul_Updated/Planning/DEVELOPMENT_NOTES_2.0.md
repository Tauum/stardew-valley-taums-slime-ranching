# Slime Daycare 2.0 Development Notes

**Status:** Reconciliation draft and single active design source. Items in **Remaining owner decisions** are not approved requirements until resolved with Taum.

**Purpose:** This is the single active development reference for product direction, gameplay rules, release scope, and technical boundaries. Read and update it when designing or implementing features. The archived v7 notes and older specs are source history; the decisions here reconcile them with the owner's later directions.

**Current work branch:** `feature/2.0-save-data`, branched from `rework-2.0`. The former feature test is paused at Stage 6; resume only when requested. See `FEATURE_TESTING_WALKTHROUGH.md` for its exact checkpoint.

## Shared design principles

> Players should wonder why Stardew never had a slime sanctuary in the first place.

- Make the mod feel native to Stardew Valley: match its tone, pixel-art style, character voices, UI conventions, pacing, and sense of discovery.
- Keep vanilla slimes and the vanilla Slime Hutch unchanged. Domesticated Ranch Slimes use the separate Slime Daycare loop.
- Features should support caring for, raising, breeding, protecting, or bonding with slimes. Keep the loop low-pressure: breeding is player-started, no daily maintenance is mandatory, and completed outcomes wait safely for the player.
- Keep the first experience easy to understand while allowing optional collection, hybrid, rarity, and companion depth.
- Limit the player to one active slime companion. Its abilities should complement Stardew progression rather than replace systems such as sprinklers or Junimo Huts.
- Keep narrative conflicts nuanced and consistent with established NPC personalities; do not present invented mod lore as vanilla canon.
- Scope filter: prefer features that make the sanctuary feel more alive and naturally part of Stardew over isolated mechanics that add friction. Review each feature by asking whether it feels plausible in Stardew and makes players care more about their slimes.
- Prefer stable gameplay services separated from map coordinates, sprites, animations, and UI. Greybox layouts and test movement/footprints before a final art pass.
- Preserve working breeder/incubator logic and existing save data during the restructure. The owner has approved migrating save-specific state from the installation-folder registry to SMAPI save data and entity `modData`; follow the explicit backup and retry-safe migration rules below.
- Initial release targets single-player. Multiplayer support remains deferred until specifically designed and tested.

## Required workflow for Stardew-specific development

Before implementing a feature that depends on Stardew behavior—especially rendering, animation, pathfinding, buildings, events, or native entity lifecycles—follow this sequence:

1. Inspect the relevant decompiled game classes as read-only reference material.
2. Identify the vanilla implementation and supported SMAPI extension points for the behavior.
3. Prefer supported SMAPI/game APIs over Harmony patches. Use Harmony only when no suitable supported extension point exists, and scope any patch narrowly.
4. Make the smallest testable change that proves the behavior.
5. Verify that change in-game before building further features on top of it; record what was verified and any remaining limits.

The decompiled source tree is reference-only: never edit it, generate files into it, or include any of it in the mod or its distributable packages. `AGENTS.md` points future development work to this required workflow; this section remains the source of truth.

## Technical and save boundaries

- Use a hybrid of SMAPI save data and entity `modData`; do not use the mod-installation-folder JSON registry for save-specific gameplay state in the 2.0 architecture. Stardew remains authoritative for native entity fields such as animal name, home, age, friendship, and native ID.
- Store save-wide jobs and progression through SMAPI save-data APIs: breeding jobs, incubator state, Rare Lab and Pool bonus state, building upgrades, furniture-slot configuration, quest/cutscene progression, and companion selection. Store identity-bound data on supported entities through `modData`: Ranch Slime genome/abilities/rarity and individual egg genome metadata. Entity metadata and save-wide state may reference stable native entity IDs as appropriate.
- Keep preferences in `config.json`, not save progression. Preserve the existing external registry as migration input only: back it up, migrate records without loss or duplication, validate reload/recovery, and make the migration safe to retry. Do not delete legacy data until migration is verified.
- Unique genome eggs must not stack with other eggs. Item `modData` can copy across split stacks and does not by itself guarantee distinct genomes won't merge, so explicitly enforce non-stacking and test inventory moves, chest transfers, save/reload, and hatch identity.
- Preserve vanilla Slime Eggs and the vanilla Egg-Press. The starter egg and domesticated breeding loop provide the initial Ranch Slimes.
- Handle save/reload, duplicate items, interrupted operations, migrations, missing entities, and uninstall/reinstall as explicit lifecycle cases. Back up save-specific mod data before destructive updates; uninstall cannot run cleanup code.
- The repository's `decompiled game` junction points to `I:\Code\Mods\Stardew-Valley\decompiled game`. Use it strictly as read-only reference material. Never edit, format, generate files into, or otherwise modify the decompiled source tree.

### Proposed gameplay/service seams

These are v7's suggested responsibilities and names, not mandated class names. Keep these boundaries even if the implementation uses different types:

- `BreedingService` / `IncubationService`: own cycle timing, selected parents, egg identity, and durable job state; they must not depend on room coordinates or sprite animation.
- `FacilityInteraction`: maps a fixed tile/action to the correct facility UI.
- `ActivityStationDefinition`: data for station/slot ID, tile footprint, approach points, participant capacity, animation ID, activity duration, and eligibility.
- `ActivityManager`: finds installed stations and owns availability, reservation, and interaction lifecycle.
- `SlimeActivityState`: wandering → choosing → approaching → interacting → wandering, with safe cancel/recovery.
- `VisualProxyRenderer`: shows selected parents and incubating eggs without moving the actual animals into specialist chambers or spawning extra roaming animals.

Map coordinates and station definitions should be data-driven so room revisions do not require rewriting gameplay logic.

## Feature inventory retained through the redesign

Unless explicitly removed above, retain the feature concepts from the older notes while changing their placement architecture and sequencing. “Retain” means keep the design in scope; it does not claim that every feature is implemented or balanced.

- **Core facilities:** breeder, incubator, Egg CryoLab, rare-offspring research laboratory, Mutagenic Pool, and Gel Refinery. These are permanent Daycare facilities rather than player-pickup items. The basic Daycare includes its breeder and incubator; the Mutagenic Pool is present but broken, and rare offspring have a 0% chance because the Rare Lab is unavailable. After construction, Robin points out the broken Pool and suggests asking Maru to repair it; the mission appears in the journal on the next wakeup. Completing Maru's repair mission is sufficient to activate the Pool; no building upgrade is required. The first Robin building upgrade separately unlocks the Egg CryoLab and Rare Lab (1% base chance). The final upgrade raises Rare Lab base chance to 5% and unlocks the Gel Refinery. The Management Desk is included in the basic Daycare at a fixed location; it is a built-in fixture, not a machine or selectable furniture slot.
- **Basic-tier furniture recipes from Marnie:** Trampoline, Slide, Cushion, Seesaw, and Heated Slime Stone. These five recipes are confirmed for the basic Daycare tier. Furniture is installed into designated non-breakable slots and managed through the Daycare Management Desk.
- **First building-upgrade furniture recipes:** Pond, Tunnel, Swing, and Lamp.
- **Final building-upgrade furniture recipes:** Fairy Lights, Ball Pit, and Washing Machine. Fairy Lights occupy one furniture slot, but their cosmetic lighting effect applies throughout the Daycare interior.
- Water Bowls and the Feeding Trough are dedicated fixed amenities, not furniture-slot items. The Feeding Trough is the sole feeding facility: it handles slime feeding and their baseline happiness/heart progression. There is no separate Compost Trough or additional juvenile-growth station. Slimes sleep naturally in the Daycare, so there is no bed furniture. Do not add a separate Playground station; individual play pieces provide its activities without the complexity of a dedicated group-play system. Wallpaper/flooring customization also remains an idea.
- **Other retained systems:** controlled breeding and incubation; color/species identity; size and maturity; shiny/rarity/mutation; favorite food; companion binding and eventual custom companion AI; collection/discovery guide; existing-item production/economy ideas; Marnie's furniture/shop concepts. Their final release phase and precise mechanics are not all approved yet.

## Prior feature rules to preserve or explicitly revise

This section captures concrete feature details from the earlier feature specifications. These are design references, not a claim that the current prototype already implements them. Where a newer owner decision conflicts, the newer decision takes precedence.

### Daycare population and activity loop

- The Daycare has 10/20/30 living-slime capacity by building tier. Count slimes assigned to breeder pens as residents. Population UI and hatch behavior must respect the cap: if the Daycare is full, incubation waits at its completion point until the player makes room; never hatch above the cap. If the player permanently deletes a slime to free capacity while full, max out the Mutagenic Pool pity meter and every incubator's Rare Lab pity meter (both Rare Labs after the second incubator is added).
- Slimes wander freely between activities. The latest owner-directed schedule is: roam for a staggered random 2–4 in-game hours; then have a 50% chance to use the nearest suitable available activity for 2–3 hours; repeat. Cap use at four activities per slime per day; after that, keep roaming. Stagger timers so residents do not all choose or use the same station together.
- Station eligibility includes reachability, compatibility, availability, and capacity. A two-slime play activity reserves both places together. A station cannot be double-booked. A slime assigned to a machine/job or companion state is ineligible for recreation. Cancel, room exit, save/load, day transition, slot replacement, and station removal must release reservations and clear temporary visual offsets safely.
- Furniture uses fixed interaction/approach points and configurable participant limits. Use visual draw offsets for bounce/poses; do not move animal collision tiles to animate. Skip or simplify offscreen animations where appropriate.

### Daycare Management Desk

The basic Daycare includes one management desk at a fixed location. It acts as the hub for Sanctuary administration:

- **Slime Registry:** View all resident slimes, their genome and other information, and what each is currently doing. Use the registry to move slimes into or out of the Daycare. When the companion feature unlocks, expose the established identity-preserving companion transfer through this registry; never clone a slime.
- **Furniture and layout:** View furniture recipes the player owns and open **Furniture Edit Mode**. Show all 3/7/15 fixed slots with their contents and empty state. From the desk, place an owned recipe into an empty slot, replace an occupied slot, or remove its furniture. Furniture stays fixed and cannot be picked up or broken directly in the room.
- **Progression:** Show the current Daycare building tier and the breeder/incubator stages it has unlocked. Robin handles Daycare building upgrades through the standard building-construction flow, like a barn upgrade; machine improvements are included with building upgrades, not purchased separately from Marnie. Marnie sells furniture recipes.
- **Slime Wiki:** Browse base, hybrid, and rare slime information, including their abilities and what those abilities do.

Keep this as one desk with clearly separated tabs or sections; it is the in-building management and information hub, not another machine or furniture slot.

### Genetics, identity, and economy references

- **Current base-color ability directions:** Red/Fighter occasionally increases the chance of double monster drops; Yellow/Revealer finds artifact spots or supported hidden clues; Blue occasionally doubles fishing trash or treasure-chest catches only; Green randomly fertilizes eligible existing crops and removes weeds; Orange can double furnace output and cooked-food output; Violet can occasionally double ore from mining; Pink very slowly heals both player health and energy. Exact probabilities, cooldowns, and eligibility limits remain for balancing. Void takes the former Collector function.
- **Green ability proposal from owner:** Randomly add fertilizer to eligible existing crops and remove weeds. The fertilizer source/type, eligible crop states, frequency/chance, and limits need balancing and Stardew API review before implementation.
- v7's example mapping also calls Blue the crop irrigator, Red the fighter, Gold a proposed 5–10% shop-discount type, Prismatic a rideable companion, and Void a storage/item-collection companion. This is historical context; the newer owner decisions supersede it. In particular, Blue only has an occasional chance to double fishing-trash and treasure-chest catches; it does not improve fish quality or irrigate crops.
- Earlier recipe map had Red+Yellow→Orange, Yellow+Blue→Green, Blue+Red→Violet, plus six named two-ability hybrids (Red-Orange, Yellow-Orange, Yellow-Green, Blue-Green, Blue-Violet, Red-Violet). This conflicts with the new “different base species make a two-ability hybrid” rule; the table is historical reference pending the answer below.
- Two base parents of the same type produce that same base type. Hybrids are limited to the two abilities defined by their type. Two mature, eligible Ranch Slimes with the same exact hybrid type are allowed to breed with each other, and their offspring is that same hybrid type; this does not stack additional abilities. A hybrid paired with a base slime is eligible and produces either the base parent's type or the same hybrid type with equal 50% probability. Two different hybrid types can breed; choose uniformly among the four base-type contributor slots (25% each). If a base type appears in both hybrids, keep both slots, so that species has two chances (50% total); e.g. Red/Blue × Red/Yellow yields Red 50%, Blue 25%, Yellow 25%. Hybrid colors blend their contributing parent colors into one body color rather than displaying the colors as separate patches. Rare slimes cannot breed.
- Egg/genome data formerly listed parent IDs, generation, primary/secondary species, visual color, size, shiny, rarity, mutation, ability IDs, and liked food. Distinct genome eggs must not stack/duplicate; breeding fixes the offspring result once, and hatching must never reroll it. Preserve ancestry fields even where inheritance is removed.
- Size is a separate axis from juvenile/adult age. The earlier visual sizes are Small, Normal, Large, Giant; a 2.5× Giant sprite was requested and implemented as a prototype. Prior genetics notes say size is not inherited; bred offspring began Normal. Keep visual size and maturity separate unless the owner revises this.
- A newly hatched Ranch Slime takes **7 in-game days after hatching** to reach maturity and become eligible for breeding. This is a separate period from the incubator's 7-day incubation, so a slime hatched from an egg normally takes about 14 in-game days from egg placement to breeding maturity. The duration is comparable to Stardew's 7-night ostrich growth and slower than chickens (3 nights), sheep (4), cows/goats/ducks (5), and rabbits (6); pigs take 10 nights.
- v7's concrete size example was a Large fighter dealing more damage but moving slower, while a Small fighter deals less damage but moves faster. This is an example of a trade-off, not a finalized combat formula; do not apply an arbitrary size bonus to every ability.
- Shiny is independent of species/color/size. The old notes proposed a rare appearance plus ~10% performance increase, while the visual prototype used a 22% brightness lift and had no stat bonus. The owner's current description calls it a rare cosmetic variant with a proposed ~10% performance increase; confirm before implementing a bonus.
- Earlier liked-food pool: Bug Meat, Slime, Bat Wing, Solar Essence, Void Essence, Joja Cola, Trash, Driftwood, Broken Glasses, Broken CD, and Soggy Newspaper. IDs live in the external genome model. Missing likes were assigned stably by slime identity. Offspring used a separately selected like.
- Earlier candidate produced-item pools: Green→Fiber/Sap; Blue→Seaweed/Green Algae; Red→Coal/Copper Ore; Yellow→Clay/Mixed Seeds; Orange→Hardwood/Wood; Violet→Iron Ore/rare Gold Ore; Pink→Wild Seeds/very rare Life Elixir. Hybrid output could draw from both parents. Rates, caps, exceptional outputs, and whether the system ships remain undecided; prevent arbitrage and do not let it replace vanilla progression.

### Companions and exceptional traits

- **Companion transfer through the Management Desk:** When companionship unlocks, use the Slime Registry to select a managed slime and take that same slime out as the player's companion; use it again to return the active companion to the Daycare. To switch companions, return the active one before selecting another. This transfers identity and state; it never clones a slime. Only one companion may be active at once, and an active companion is unavailable for breeding, furniture, or jobs. This replaces a separate Companion Register installation and the older Slime Core item-binding proposal.
- Implement custom movement/pathfinding, obstacle handling, stuck recovery, location transitions, action selection, cooldowns and inventory safety—not a vanilla follower reskin. Start single-player and same-location before adding transitions. Explain the one-active limit in the desk/selection UI and clearly show which companion is active.
- Current base-color ability directions: Red/Fighter occasionally increases the chance of double monster drops; Yellow/Revealer finds artifact spots or supported hidden clues; Blue occasionally doubles fishing-trash or treasure-chest catches only; Green randomly fertilizes eligible existing crops and removes weeds; Orange can double furnace output and cooked-food output; Violet can occasionally double ore from mining; Pink very slowly restores player health and energy. Exact probabilities, cooldowns, and eligibility limits remain to be balanced. The old animal-happiness effect is removed from Orange. Hybrids use two abilities with separate cooldowns/caps. These are design decisions, not implemented behavior.
- Exceptional concepts: Gold/Golden has a strict capped shop discount; Void stores an additional row of items it picks up when the player's inventory is full; Prismatic is rideable at any size, and the player can select its other/base role ability. Black extends the normal day by one hour to 3 AM; White starts the day one hour early at 5 AM. Black and White are confirmed concepts but their implementation remains deferred until after the core Sanctuary/gameplay systems because of overnight, schedule, event, and mod-compatibility risks. Shadow is removed from the current design. Shiny, exceptional species, size and mutation should remain distinct genome fields. V7 recommends that riding use the one active companion slot; this remains an owner decision below.
- Enforce the one-companion limit centrally on load, dismissal/replacement, and any future multiplayer/location transitions; inactive slimes must not continue field abilities.
- Apply ability-specific caps and size scaling rather than a global multiplier. Small was associated with speed; Large/Giant with area/quantity, but discounts and time boundaries should not scale by size. Any future day-boundary ability needs safeguards for sleep/day transitions and events, and remains deferred as noted above. Companion acquisition, Core slot, dismissal token, and exact limits are deferred until the companion release.

## Decisions recorded from owner review (2026-10-28)

- **Machines:** Convert current machines from pickup-able items into permanent, dedicated facility placements, following the vanilla Slime Incubator / Autofeeder interaction model. Players cannot pick them up or break them. Preserve the systems and features already outlined; this changes their placement model, not their purpose.
- **Furniture:** Keep the existing furniture and machine features in scope. The Daycare has three selectable ground slots initially, seven after the first building upgrade, and 15 after the final upgrade. This intentionally prevents players from fitting the full furniture catalog until the final building tier. Basic recipes are Trampoline, Slide, Cushion, Seesaw, and Heated Slime Stone; the first upgrade unlocks Pond, Tunnel, Swing, and Lamp; the final upgrade unlocks Fairy Lights, Ball Pit, and Washing Machine. Fairy Lights occupy one furniture slot but apply their cosmetic lighting effect across the Daycare interior. Water Bowls and the Feeding Trough are dedicated fixed amenities outside the furniture slots; the Feeding Trough handles feeding and baseline happiness/heart progression. Robin handles Daycare building upgrades; after each Robin upgrade, Marnie visits, comments on the player's progress, and announces newly available recipes. Placed furniture cannot be picked up or broken. The Management Desk's Furniture Edit Mode lets players edit or replace slot contents.
- **Daycare interior layout:** Use one continuous interior map with no room transitions. The central playroom is the accessible roaming/furniture area. The breeder projects from its top wall behind glass, starting as a 4×4-tile greybox and expanding to 5×4 or 6×4 only if visibility, equipment, or animation clearance requires it. Players click the glass-fronted installation from the playroom and cannot enter the chamber. Show selected parents as lightweight animated visual representations, not independently pathfinding animals. Incubators and other specialist facilities are fixed, visible installations with playroom-accessible interaction points. Reserve space for the fully upgraded layout; the final breeder upgrade adds a second pen and expands the existing area by approximately 2–3 tiles. Keep chamber/map coordinates, dimensions, and interaction points data-driven. Use [breeder picture.png](../breeder%20picture.png) as a greybox visual reference; its other room/facility dimensions remain proposals unless separately approved.
- **Breeding:** Two base slimes of the same type produce that base type. Two different base species produce a two-ability hybrid. Two parents that share the exact same hybrid type can reproduce and produce that same hybrid type. A hybrid/base pairing is eligible and produces the base parent's type or the same hybrid type with equal 50% probability. Two different hybrids produce one of their four base-type contributor slots, chosen uniformly at 25% per slot; duplicate base types keep both slots and their probabilities add together. Rare slimes cannot breed.
- **Introduction:** Marnie gives the player one item named **Domesticated Slime Egg**, described as **“The last known to exist.”** On the night it is due to hatch, a Fairy-event-like dream shows a slime-cell dividing into two. On waking, consume the egg and create two distinct Common Ranch Slimes in the Daycare, prompting the player to name each slime independently. The Wizard's potion explodes on him and is ruined. The Daycare stays secret by remaining on the farm; companions are unavailable until much later.
- **Core pacing/capacity targets:** Basic Daycare: 10-slime capacity, one 18-day breeder, and one 7-day incubator. First Robin building upgrade: 20-slime capacity and the single breeder improves to 14 days. Final Robin building upgrade: 30-slime capacity, adds a second 14-day breeder pen and a second separate 7-day incubator, with its own adjacent Rare Lab. There are no incubator speed tiers. Building upgrades include these machine improvements; do not sell them separately. Do not silently reset dates of cycles already in progress during migration.
- **Rare and mutation systems:** Keep the rare-offspring research lab separate from the Mutagenic Pool. ### Technical implementation rules retained from the earlier contract

- **Native versus mod state:** Stardew owns the FarmAnimal's native ID, name, home, age, and friendship. SMAPI save data owns save-wide breeding/incubation jobs, research bonuses, upgrades, furniture-slot configuration, quest progress, and companion selection. Supported entity `modData` owns identity-bound Ranch Slime genomes and individual egg genomes. Keep `config.json` for preferences only.
- **Save metadata and migration:** Do not extend the installation-folder registry as the 2.0 persistence architecture. Migrate existing external records into the hybrid SMAPI save-data/entity-`modData` model with backup, retry safety, and verification before retiring the old records. Unique genome eggs require explicit non-stacking rules because item `modData` may copy across split stacks and may not prevent unlike eggs from merging. Verify chest/inventory transfers, save/reload, and exact hatch identity.
- **Lifecycle and safety:** Keep recovery for save-data migrations. Reconcile registered animals to live native IDs. Removed animals are detached from active identity without erasing their genome archive. Missing-parent unfinished jobs clear safely; already-produced outputs remain recoverable. Make every transaction atomic against duplicate item/resource creation. Uninstall cannot clean up save-specific data; explain backup/recovery limits.
- **Vanilla boundaries:** Inspect the decompiled implementation and SMAPI extension points before Stardew-specific changes; prefer supported APIs to Harmony and narrowly scope any necessary patch by location and entity. Preserve the Slime Hutch, wild/mine/Secret Woods GreenSlimes, vanilla Slime Egg items/drops/treasure, and vanilla Egg-Press. No global slime behavior changes. The custom Daycare and Ranch Slime animal are separate systems.
- **Service boundaries:** Keep save-state/lifecycle, genetics, breeding, egg identity, incubation, fixed facility interaction adapters, furniture/activity scheduler, mutation/pity, production/refinery, companion lifecycle, and time effects in separate services. Map coordinates, tile footprints, sprites, animations, and UI are data/adapters; gameplay services must survive map/art changes.
- **Time and random outcomes:** Resolve random outcomes once on the authoritative job start/completion step, persist the chosen result and ready date, and never reroll after reload. Keep day-boundary effects isolated and guarded. Multiplayer is deferred; if reconsidered, host owns rolls, inventory transactions, and machine state.
- **Visual/runtime performance:** Reuse cached textures and precomputed station occupancy where practical; keep animations transient and restore draw offsets on completion, room exit, title return, machine reservation, or slot removal. Test a crowded 30-slime Daycare before finalizing visual work.
- **Build and artifact workflow:** The mod uses C#/SMAPI on Stardew 1.6+. Placeholder art and a greybox layout come first; generated/revised pixel art needs correct sprite-sheet dimensions, origins, collision, layering, and in-game verification. Exterior design selection is deferred. The `decompiled game` junction is read-only and must never receive writes.

## 2.0 implementation checklist

This is the working development sequence derived from the decisions above. Keep each feature isolated on its own branch, branched from the previous accepted 2.0 feature, and merge it into `rework-2.0` only after its in-game acceptance check passes. Do not treat unresolved items below as permission to invent requirements. Before changing Stardew-specific rendering, animation, pathfinding, buildings, events, or native entity lifecycles, inspect the relevant read-only decompiled classes, identify vanilla behavior and supported SMAPI extension points, prefer APIs over Harmony, make the smallest testable change, and verify it in-game. Never include decompiled source in the distributable mod.

## Remaining owner decisions

1. **Release order:** Companions are confirmed for much later. Use v7's three-release order as the working proposal: core Sanctuary/story/breeding; then living Daycare/expansions/furniture/research; then companions. Retained systems are deferred rather than removed.
2. **Upgrade dependency and construction costs:** The capacity and machine progression is set: basic (capacity 10) has one 18-day breeder, one 7-day incubator, and a broken Mutagenic Pool; first upgrade (capacity 20) improves the breeder to 14 days and unlocks the Egg CryoLab, 1% Rare Lab, and a Maru-led mission to repair the Pool; final upgrade (capacity 30) adds a second 14-day breeder, a second 7-day incubator with its own Rare Lab at 5%, and the Gel Refinery. Confirmed costs are 10,000g + 350 Wood + 150 Stone + 50 Slime for the basic Daycare; 25,000g + 450 Wood + 200 Stone + 100 Slime for the first upgrade; and 100,000g + 550 Wood + 300 Stone + 200 Slime for the final upgrade. Define the spatial room expansion for each tier.
3. **Opening event trigger:** The main Lewis → Wizard → Marnie → Robin → starter split → Wizard follow-up → first player-started breeding sequence is mandatory to deliver the starter egg and teach the loop. Specify the save/day/season conditions for Lewis's first visit and the safe trigger window/location for Marnie's post-construction visit; the later milestone interactions are optional.
4. **Specialist facility dimensions and map art:** The continuous-map layout, top-wall glass-front breeder, 4×4 starting greybox, click-from-playroom interaction, player exclusion from chambers, visual proxies, and future 2–3-tile expansion are approved. Decide the incubator and other specialist-facility footprints, final art/layering standards, and any overall room dimensions before final map/art work. The other measurements shown in `breeder picture.png` remain visual proposals until approved.
5. **Ability scope:** Which proposed abilities are feasible and balanced for the first companion release, and does riding use the same one-companion slot? Companion switching uses the Management Desk's Slime Registry: return the active slime and select its replacement.
6. **Story scene detail:** The potion explosion victim is the Wizard. Its intended effect, exact comic consequence, dialogue, and how much the Guild learns are still writing details to settle without contradicting that decision. The opening quest is mandatory; milestone character interactions after the first-breeding tutorial are optional.
7. **Quest milestones:** Confirm the post-Marnie tutorial completion condition and ensure quest progression remains recoverable if the player misses a visit or skips the split event. The Rare Lab unlocks with the first Robin building upgrade; it is not gated by a first hybrid or first rare slime. After Daycare construction, Robin points the player to Maru; her Mutagenic Pool repair mission is added to the journal on the next wakeup. Deliver 1 Battery Pack, 5 Iron Bars, and 10 Refined Quartz to complete it and activate the Pool, with no building upgrade requirement.
