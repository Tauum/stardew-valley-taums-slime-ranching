# Stage 4 — Furniture sold by Marnie

Placeable hutch furniture bought from Marnie. Build reusable interaction points, size eligibility, cooldowns, reservations, and short animations. Trampoline compresses and launches slimes with sprite vertical offset; larger slimes compress it more. Also: beds, slide, tunnel, ball pit, swing, lamp, cushion, playground, heated stone, Gel Pond, compost trough. Mostly aesthetic; modest happiness/juvenile maturation effects where explicitly specified. No elaborate optimization economy. Furniture persists across save/reload and doesn't affect wild slimes.
