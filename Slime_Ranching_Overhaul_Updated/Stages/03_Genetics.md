'# Stage 3 - Color genetics and species abilities

Breeding passes on color/species identity only through the defined color recipes. Ranch Slime egg icons use the same rendered color variant as the resulting slime. Ability identifiers are derived from the offspring species; hybrid species may have two base ability IDs. Size, rarity, shiny state, mutation, and favorite food are not inherited: offspring start at Normal, Common, non-shiny, no mutation, and receive a newly assigned favorite food. Parent IDs and generation remain as ancestry metadata. Size and other visual traits remain individually configurable.

**Done when:** color recipes produce the intended species/color, egg tint matches the hatched slime, species-derived ability identifiers are stored, and these values survive save/reload without non-color traits passing from parents.
